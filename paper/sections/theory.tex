\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

Consider a space-time domain, $\X = A \times \T \subset \mathbb{S}^2 \times \R$ with elements $x \in \X$, where $x = (s,t)$, for location 
$s \in A$ and $t \in \T$. For simplicity, let the time domain $\T$ be composed of an integer number of years $\t_1, \ldots, \t_m$. Argo floats sample a spatialtemporally continuous ocean heat content (OHC) $S(x)$ at a discrete set of spacetime points $x_i, i = 1,\ldots\,n$, where $S = \{S(x): x \in \X\}$ denotes the unobserved continuous ohc process on the domain.  

A simple statistical model for sampling the ohc field is,
\begin{equation}
  Y_i \;=\; S(x_i) + \eta_i, \qquad i=1,\ldots,n, 
\end{equation}
where $Y_i$ denotes the measured value--the vertically integrated OHC--at spacetime point $x_i$, and $\eta_i$ are mutually independent, zero-mean random variables with (nugget) varaince $\tau^2$.

Further, a common model for the true ohc field is, 
\begin{equation}
  S(x_i) \;=\; \mu(x_i) + a(x_i) + \e(x_i), \qquad i=1,\ldots,n, 
\end{equation}
where $\mu$ is the climatological mean field, $a$ is a zero-mean Argo resolvable anomaly field, and $\e$ is a zero-mean unresolvable field from fine-scale variation.

\begin{assumption}
\label{ass:efield}
$\E(\e) = 0,\; \Cov(\e(x_i), \e(x_{i'})) = 0,\; \forall\; x_i \ne x_{i'}$.
\end{assumption}

Let $X = \{x_1,\dots,x_n\}$ be the set of sampling points, $S(X) = \{S(x_1),\ldots,S(x_n)\}$ the value of the process at the sampling points, and $Y=(Y_1,\ldots,Y_n)$ the measured values of the process at the sampling points. We consider the joint distribution of the process $S$, the measurement points $X$, and the measured values $Y$, $[S, X, Y]$.

If $X$ is a random point process on $\X$ that is \emph{not} independent of $S$, the design is
said to be \textbf{preferential}, i.e., $[S,X] \ne [S][X]$. The joint distribution factorizes as
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:preffac}
  [S, X, Y] \;=\; [S]\,[X \mid S]\,[Y \mid X, S] \;=\; [S]\,[X \mid S]\,[Y \mid S(X)].
\end{equation}

Preferential sampling is characterized by dependence between the random sampling process realization $X$ and the measured process $S$.

The goal of this work is to test the existence of preferential sampling of Argo floats on ocean heat content and estimate the preferential sampling bias induced by averaging biased samples.

Consider the estimand of expected ocean heat content over the domain $\X$,
\begin{equation}
  \ohcbar = \int_{\X} S(x) \dd \P(x)
\end{equation}
where $\P$ is a uniform probability measure normalized over spacetime.
The Argo observation system induce an empirical sampling measure $\Q_n = \frac1n\sum_i\delta_{x_i}$ with limiting distribution $\Q$.
Accordingly, the estimated ocean heat content with respect to the sampling system is,
\begin{equation}
  \ohchat = \int_{\X} S(x) \dd \Q(x),
\end{equation}
and Argo sampling induced bias is 
\begin{equation}
  B = \ohchat - \ohcbar = \int_{\X} S(x) \dd (\Q - \P)(x).
\end{equation}

Critically, the total bias can be non-zero even when $\Q = \P$ due to preferential sampling.
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:biasdecomp}
  B
  \;=\; \underbrace{\int_{\X} \mu(x) \, d(\Q - \P)(x)}_{\textbf{coverage}} \;+\;
        \underbrace{\int_{\X} a(x) \, d(\Q - \P)(x)}_{\textbf{preferential}} \;+\;
      \underbrace{\int_{\X} \e(x) \, d(\Q - \P)(x)}_{\textbf{preferential}}.
\end{equation}

Coverage bias results from $\Q \ne \P$, i.e., non-uniform sampling of the climatological mean field; whereas, preferential sampling arises when the sampling positions $X$ which define $Q$ are dependent on the anomaly field $a$ and the fine-scale field $\e$. 

However, we do not observe $S(X)$ but rather $Y(X) = S(X) + \eta$ at finite points $X = x_1,\ldots,x_n$. Therefore, in order to test for the existence of preferential sampling bias we approximate the continuous field $S$ with a full observed proxy field $\tS: \X \to \R$. The reanalysis product GLORYS12V1 provides a suitable proxy of ohc on a $1/12^{\circ}$ (approximately 8 km) grid as well as a consistent velocity field that can be used to advect synthetic Argo floats using Lagragian simulation.

Our first goal is to conduct a permutation test to evaluate the null hypothesis of no preferential sampling. To construct a valid hypothesis test we will first construct a test statistic $T$ that is centered under the null of non-preferential sampling, such that deviations of $T$ from 0 will give evidence against the null.
Our procedure will generate a sampling distribution for the realized preferential bias under the dynamics generated by $\tS$. 

Consider the partition of the domain $\X$ into $C \in \N$ discrete cells $\X = \bigcup_j c_j$, with spacetime volume $P(c_j) = p_j$. The ohc field $\tS$ is constant within cell and can be written as a simple function $\tS(x) = \sum_j \tS_j \1\{x \in c_j\}$, where $\tS_j = \tS(x)$, for $x \in c_j$. Under finite sampling of $\tS$, the empirically sampling mass on cell $c_j$ is $w_j = Q_n(c_j) = \frac1n \sum_i \1\{x_i \in c_j\} = \frac{n_j}{n}$, where $n_j$ is the number of profiles in cell $c_j$. Note, $\sum_j w_j = \sum_j p_j = 1$. Thus, the observed sampling bias within the proxy field is
\begin{equation}
  \tB \;=\; \int_{\X} \tS(x) \dd (\Q_n -\P)(x) \;=\; \sum_{j = 1}^C (w_j - p_j) \tS_j.
\end{equation}

It is helpful to conceptualize the sampling design $X$ inducing a linear functional
\begin{equation}
  L(f) = \sum_{j = 1}^C (w_j - p_j) f_j,
\end{equation}
such that under uniform and non-preferential sampling $L(f) = 0$ meaning the sampling design reproduces the mean of $f$.
Then, $\tB = L(\tS)$.

Under the same statistical model, $\tS(x_i) \;=\; \tilde{\mu}(x_i) + \tilde{a}(x_i) + \tilde{\e}(x_i), \; i=1,\ldots,n$, where $\tilde{a}$ and $\tilde{\e}$ are zero-mean anomaly and zero-mean fine-scale fields respectively, such that 
\begin{equation}
  \tB = L(\tS_j) = L(\tilde{\mu}_j + \tilde{a}_j + \tilde{\e}_j).
\end{equation}

It is now clear that if the sampling design is independent of the anomaly and fine-scale fields then $\E[w_j(\tilde{a}_j + \tilde{\e}_j)] = \E(w_j)\E(\tilde{a}_j + \tilde{\e}_j) = 0$ and the expected sampling bias reduces to the expected coverage bias: $\E(L(\tilde{\mu}_j))$. Where expectation is with respect to the joint distribution of the anomaly field and the sampling design. Accordingly, under non-preferential sampling the expected sampling bias of the mean centered field $\tS - \tilde{\mu}$ is 0.

Practically, we estimate the mean field, including its seasonality and drift, as $\hat{m}$ and estimate the zero-mean residual field as $\tZ(x) = \tS(x) - \hat{m}(x)$. Denote $\tZ_k$ the residual proxy field restricted to year $\mathbf{t}_k$.

Let $\tV = \{\tV(x):\, x \in \X\}$ be the proxy velocity field from the reanalysis product, where $\tV_k$ is the velocity field in year $\mathbf{t}_k$. Define $\D$ to be the joint distribution of float deployment spacetime positions mission parameters, lifespans, and failure times. Let $\omega \sim \D$ a realization of deployment positions.
Further, let $\G: (\omega, v) \mapsto X \subset \X$ be a lagragian simulation map that advects floats seeded according to $\omega$ through the velocity field $v$ and returns the resulting profile positions $X$.

Denote $X^{(l)} = \G(\omega,\, \tV_l)$ be the sampling design resulting from an arbitrary deployment realization $\omega$ and velocity field $\tV_l$.
Each design inducecs an empirical sampling measure $\Q_n^{(l)}$ with sampling cell weights $w_j^{(l)} = \frac{1}{n^{(l,d)}} \sum_i \1\{x_i^{(l,d)} \in c_j\}$, where $\sum_j w_j^{(l)} = 1$ over the cells $c_j$.
Then, consider the linear function $L^{(l,d)}(f) = \sum_{j=1}^{C}(w_j^{(l)} - p_j)f_j$, such that $B_k^{(l)} = L^{(l,d)}(\tZ_k)$ is the sampling bias incurred when floats advected by the velocity field of year $\t_l$ sample the proxy residual OHC field of year $\t_k$.

Note, because $\tS$ is constant within cells, $B_k^{(l)}$ depends on the sampling design only through the weight vector $w^{(l)}$.


Under the following assumptions we can construct a valid permutation test for preferential sampling.
Throughout, we index the $m$ analysis years by $k$ and the $M \ge m$ velocity years available in the reanalysis record by $l$.
For a target number of deployment simulations $D$, for each pair $(l,d)$ with $l = 1,\ldots,M$ and $d = 1,\ldots,D$, we will draw $\omega_{l,d} \overset{iid}{\sim} \D$ which induces cell sampling weights $w^{(l,d)}$.

\begin{assumption}[D0: deployment exogeneity]
\label{as:D0}
Float deployment positions, mission parameters, lifespans and failure times do not depend on
the ocean state; that is, $\D$ is not a function of $\tZ$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[D1a: constant design mean]
\label{as:D1a}
There exists $\bar{w} \in \R^{C}$ such that $\E\big[w_j^{(l)}\big] = \bar{w}_j$ for every velocity year $l$ and every cell $j = 1,\ldots,C$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[D1b: design invariance]
\label{as:D1b}
The cell weight vectors are identically distributed across velocity years,
\begin{equation}
  w^{(l)} \;\overset{d}{=}\; w^{(l')}, \qquad l, l' = 1,\ldots,M .
\end{equation}
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[D2: cross-year independence]
\label{as:D2}
For every $l \in \C_k := \{l : |l - k| \ge 2\}$, the weights $w^{(l)}$ are independent of $\tZ_k$.
\end{assumption}


As stated, preferential sampling occurs when the sampling design, as characterized by a sampling weight vector $w$, is not independent of the residual OHC field $\tZ$.
Therefore, our primary null hypothesis is that the sampling vectors $w^{(k)}$ are independent of $\tZ_k$ for all years $k$,

\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:H0}
  H_0: \quad w^{(k)} \;\perp\; \tZ_k, \qquad k = 1,\ldots,m .
\end{equation}


\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:iid}
Under Assumptions~\ref{as:D0}--\ref{as:D2} and $H_0$, conditional on $\{\tZ_k\}_{k=1}^{m}$
the $MD$ weight vectors $\{w^{(l,d)}\}$ are independent and identically distributed.
Consequently, for each analysis year $k$, the scalars $\{B_k^{(l,d)}\}$ are independent and
identically distributed, and in particular exchangeable under arbitrary permutation of the
velocity-year labels.
\end{lemma}

Let $\lambda(l,d) = l$ denote the true velocity-year label of design $(l,d)$, and for a
labelling $\lambda$ define
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:Tk}
  T_k(\lambda) \;=\; \frac{1}{D}\sum_{(l,d):\,\lambda(l,d) = k} B_k^{(l,d)}
  \;-\; \frac{1}{|\C_k|D}\sum_{(l,d):\,\lambda(l,d) \in \C_k} B_k^{(l,d)} ,
\end{equation}
the difference between the mean bias among designs labelled as year $k$ and the mean among all
others, and let $\bar{T}(\lambda) = m^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^{m} T_k(\lambda)$. The observed statistics
$T_k^{\obs}$ and $\bar{T}^{\obs}$ are evaluated at the true labelling.

By Lemma~\ref{lem:iid} the labels are exchangeable under $H_0$, so a null replicate is obtained
by drawing $\lambda^{(r)}$ uniformly from the labellings assigning exactly $D$ designs to each
of the $M$ velocity years, and recomputing \eqref{eq:Tk}. A single relabelling is applied to all
$m$ analysis years, preserving the dependence among $\{T_k\}$. With $N$ such replicates,
\begin{equation}
  \hat{p}_{\mathrm{pool}}
  \;=\; \frac{1 + \#\big\{r : \bar{T}(\lambda^{(r)}) \ge \bar{T}^{\obs}\big\}}{1 + N} .
\end{equation}
For year-specific inference, standardise by the permutation standard deviation
$\hat{\sigma}_k$ of $T_k$, set
$\Lambda^{(r)} = \max_{k} T_k(\lambda^{(r)})/\hat{\sigma}_k$, and report
\begin{equation}
  \hat{p}_k \;=\; \frac{1 + \#\big\{r : \Lambda^{(r)} \ge T_k^{\obs}/\hat{\sigma}_k\big\}}{1 + N} ,
\end{equation}
which control the familywise error rate without further correction.
